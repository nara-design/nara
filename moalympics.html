Introduction
A unique challenge of unfixed branding

Moalympics is a fan-led initiative consisting of a group of volunteers who host streaming parties on Stationhead and Weverse in support of the artist “TOMORROW X TOGETHER” (also known as TXT). These streaming parties allow fans of TXT to gather in one place to enjoy and listen to music together, chat with each other, and on some occasions even get joined by the members of TXT themselves.

For every new album released by TXT, their visual identity receives an update, and Moalympics likewise refreshes their look to match. My role within this was to design the social media posts to promote Moalympics’ streaming parties, while keeping in alignment with each album’s visual identity.
