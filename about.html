Who am I?

A learner at heart

I’m a Christchurch-based digital designer who enjoys learning and creating things. I thrive on variety + challenges, so I love going beyond my existing skill-set to combine all sorts of mediums, tools & experiences in my work. As a result, I have dipped into 3D animation and VFX, 2D animation, web design, web development, brand design, illustration, and more!

Designing to address problems

Though my strengths lie in UX/UI design, I also have a strong foundation in more traditional mediums (drawing, painting, handcraft) and I'm always willing to explore other fields, especially those that may work better to solve the unique problems within each project. As much as I like creating graphics and web-app experiences, some design problems need other solutions, and I enjoy creating those just as much.
